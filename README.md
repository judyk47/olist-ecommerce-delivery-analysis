# Olist E-Commerce Delivery Analysis

## Overview
Analyze the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to investigate sales trends, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.

## Questions
1. How do orders, revenue, and average order value change over time?
2. What share of orders arrive late, and how does it vary by state, category, and seller?
3. How are delivery delays related to review scores?
4. Which categories and sellers combine high revenue with poor delivery or satisfaction?
5. How do freight costs and delivery-estimate accuracy relate to satisfaction?

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab, and KaggleHub.

## Workflow
- Load and inspect dataset tables.
- Check missing values, duplicates, relationships, and date inconsistencies.
- Clean dates, labels, and product categories.
- Validate cleaned data.
- Analyze sales, delivery times, freight, and reviews.
- Develop business recommendations.

## Initial Findings
- Median delivery time: 10 days.
- Average delivery time: 12.1 days.
- 77.1% of reviews received four or five stars.
- 7.5% of items exceeded the IQR price threshold.
- 610 products had missing category labels.

## Status
Data loading, quality checks, cleaning, and initial exploration are complete. Further analysis is needed to answer all five business questions.

## Dataset
[Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)
